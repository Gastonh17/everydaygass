    RPE and running zones

    Sessions use feeling, not a race pace. Learn the terms, then set zones if you train with a watch.

    RPE is rate of perceived exertion — how hard the work feels from 1 to 10. Match the number on the session, not your ego.

    1. 1–2
      Recovery

      Barely working. Easy walk, mobility. You could do this all day.

    2. 3–4
      Easy · you can talk

      Full sentences. Easy runs live here. If you can’t speak, slow down.

    3. 5–6
      Moderate · steady

      Short sentences. Quality or hybrid work. Honest, still in control.

    4. 7
      Hard

      A few words. Strength last reps, HYROX rounds. Leave 1–2 reps in the tank.

    5. 8–10
      Very hard / max
