Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Who we are

EverydayGass (“we”) provides sports and lifestyle coaching. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you use this website or contact us.

What we collect

When you fill in the booking form we collect your name, email, stated goal, training history, and how many days you can train. If you email us, we also process whatever you choose to send.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and book a call
  • To deliver coaching if you become a client
  • To run and improve this website

How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept only as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted unless you become a client or the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, or delete your data, or object to certain processing. Write to hello@everydaygass.com.
